## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, editorial identity for PT Cipta Ruang Nusantara. Rooted in the poise of contemporary spatial design, high-end architectural documentation, and bespoke atelier curation, the interface reflects quiet luxury, structural rigor, and deliberate restraint.

### Brand Personality & Mood
- **Architectural & Monolithic:** Solid, grounding, and deliberate. Surfaces read as physical materials—fluted stone, honed obsidian, matte composite panels, and smoked glass.
- **Quiet Authority:** Confident restraint. The interface refrains from ornamental gradients, loud micro-animations, or transient tech trends. Content and spatial forms dominate.
- **Artisanal Human Precision:** Evokes Swiss layout discipline and architectural drafting tables: strict alignments, asymmetric visual weight, hairline precision, and intentional negative space.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **Dark Editorial Luxury** and **Structural Swiss Modernism**. Visual hierarchy is governed by proportion, scale contrasts, and tonal density rather than drop shadows or heavy chromatic fills.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw architectural matter: basalt, charcoal, smoked iron, oxidized brick, and warm amber task-lighting.

### Surface Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#0E0F12`):** Primary ambient void. Dense, matte obsidian avoiding pure `#000000` to preserve chromatic depth.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#14161B`):** Structural panels, sidebars, and grouping regions.
- **Interactive Container (`#1B1E24`):** Cards, input beds, and floating panels.
- **Overlaid Surface (`#242831`):** Hover states, popovers, and elevated dialogs.

### Accent Hierarchy
- **Primary Accent (`#D97736` - Burnt Amber):** Echoes warm halogen spot-illumination against cold materials. Reserved for active states, key CTAs, and primary focal highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8C2723` - Terracotta Crimson):** Grounded earth and iron oxide. Applied selectively for tags, status indicators, and secondary highlights.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#C5A880` - Travertine Sand):** Muted metallic buff used for subtle badges, key architectural dimensions, and subdued metadata.

### Typographic Contrast
- **Text High-Emphasis (`#F4F4F6`):** Pure alabaster for titles and vital values.
- **Text Mid-Emphasis (`#A0A4AB`):** Muted silver for body copy and continuous reading.
- **Text Low-Emphasis (`#5C626C`):** Architectural graphite for captions, hairline dimensions, and metadata.

## Typography

Typography functions as the primary visual texture of the interface. Editorial clarity governs every scale:

- **Primary Headings & Monolithic Marks:** `Space Grotesk` introduces geometric balance, architectural framing, and engineered modernity with zero visual clutter.
- **Continuous Reading & Interface Copy:** `Metrophobic` delivers clean, open-aperture geometry that retains immaculate legibility across low-contrast and dark-mode planes.
- **Technical Readouts & Architectural Labels:** All micro-metadata, indices, coordinate tokens, and category flags run in uppercase `Space Grotesk` with wide letter spacing (+0.08em to +0.1em) mimicking drafting annotations and architectural blueprints.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model honors classical spatial ratios and modern gallery design principles. Generous negative space frames high-density project records.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column asymmetric fluid grid with `5rem` page margins and `2.5rem` structural gutters. Sections feature expansive margins to let project captures breathe.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` outer margins. Dynamic stacks replace horizontal multi-column structures.

### Structural Rhythm
- Element separation follows strict modular multiples of `0.25rem` (4px baseline).
- Section compositions use generous vertical offsets (`space-xl` scaled up to `6rem` for editorial transitions) to evoke a physical exhibition catalog.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved exclusively through material tone differentiation and pristine hairline perimeter lines, eliminating heavy artificial dropshadows.

### Material Tiers
1. **Base Plane (`#0E0F12`):** Primary canvas underpinning all content.
2. **Structural Enclosures (`#14161B`):** Modules framed with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`.
3. **Card Plates (`#1B1E24`):** Interactive containers with top edge highlight lines (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) and lateral/bottom hairline borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
4. **Floating Overlays & Modals (`#242831`):** Floating modules leverage subtle ambient diffusion: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
5. **Glass Accents:** Translucent frosted planes apply `background: rgba(20, 22, 27, 0.82)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a crisp `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` perimeter.

## Shapes

The design system employs an understated, micro-softened shape grammar. Geometry remains sharp, controlled, and architectural, avoiding rounded pill forms and generic bubble interfaces.

- **Containers & Surfaces:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii across cards, panels, inputs, and interactive dialogs.
- **Badges & Micro-tags:** `0.125rem` (2px) or sharp `0px` to mirror physical brass catalog plaques and architectural scale markers.
- **Buttons & Action Triggers:** Strict `0.25rem` (4px) matching primary containers for unified structural integrity.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#D97736` background with `#0E0F12` text in medium weight `Space Grotesk`. Hover state darkens slightly to `#C2652B` with zero elevation shift.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `transparent`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#F4F4F6`. On hover, the border shifts to `#F4F4F6` and background shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`.
- **Text Action:** Unbordered, uppercase, tracked typography accompanied by a micro rule line or mono directional glyph (`→`).

### Cards & Presentation Containers
- Matte `#1B1E24` surfaces encased in hairline strokes (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`).
- Padding strictly adheres to `space-lg` (desktop) and `space-md` (mobile).
- Interactive cards elevate subtly via border illumination to `rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.4)` rather than scaling or shifting position.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Bed surface of `#14161B` with a bottom-anchored or full-stroke hairline border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`). Active focus state transitions the border to `#D97736` with zero glow blur.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Minimalist square frames (`16px`) with razor-thin borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`). Selected states flood with `#D97736` and display an obsidian check or center dot.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- Compact, flat structures with `#14161B` fill, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` perimeter, and `0.6875rem` uppercase label typography.
- Status and discipline variants introduce micro-dots (2px solid circles) rendered in `#D97736` (Architecture), `#8C2723` (Spatial Planning), or `#C5A880` (Material Lab).

### Lists & Blueprint Tables
- Dividers between records are drawn as `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- Rows feature subtle hover highlighting (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`) and monospace drafting-style index numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`) set in `Space Grotesk`.

### Architectural Metadata Blocks
- Dedicated key-value groupings displaying project specifications (Scale, Location, Scope, Materials) structured with muted uppercase labels directly above alabaster-toned values.
